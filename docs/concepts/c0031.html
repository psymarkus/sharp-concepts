Definition

Motivational-affective learning processes are dynamic, situation-specific changes in learners’ expectancy and value beliefs and affective states. These processes unfold over time and are manifested in approach or avoidance behaviors, thereby shaping learners’ engagement with the learning activity and their future engagement with similar tasks.
