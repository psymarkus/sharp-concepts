Definition

Structure of knowledge refers to the organization and interconnectedness of knowledge elements within a professional’s knowledge base. Highly structured knowledge is characterized by encapsulation, which are meaningful relationships among concepts, principles, procedures, and situation-specific scripts that support efficient reasoning and action.
