Definition

Representational scaffolding is the systematic selection and modification of practice representations in simulations in order to adjust the level of difficulty to learners with different learning prerequisites and learning goals in order to facilitate the acquisition of knowledge and skills needed to successfully tackle the represented situations of practice. The main representational features adjusted by RS are informational complexity, typicality, agency, and situation dynamics
