Personalization

IRI

Identifier

c0034

Preferred label

Personalization

Alternative labels

No alternative labels available.

Definition

Personalization refers to the data-informed adjustment of instructional conditions, support, or learning experiences to characteristics, needs, or learning states of an individual learner in order to improve learning processes and outcomes.
