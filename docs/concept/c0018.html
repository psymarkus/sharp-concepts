Intervention activities

Preferred label

Intervention activities

Alternative labels

No alternative labels available.

Definition

Intervention activities refer to observable actions that externalize the processes underlying intervening, such as generating, selecting, implementing, communicating, and evaluating options for action in practice. Sequences of intervention activities may provide evidence about underlying intervention processes.
