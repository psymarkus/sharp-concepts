Learning prerequisites

IRI

https://w3id.org/sharp/concept/c0022

Identifier

c0022

Preferred label

Learning prerequisites

Alternative labels

No alternative labels available.

Definition

Learning prerequisites refer to learner characteristics that are present before or at the beginning of a learning activity and that influence subsequent learning processes and learning outcomes.
