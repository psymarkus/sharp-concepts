Definition

Intervening refers to the professional practice of influencing, supporting, or changing conditions, behaviours, or developmental trajectories of clients, with the aim of identifying and optimizing appropriate and effective responses to a given problem. Intervening may occur individually or collaboratively. It describes the broader phenomenon of intervention rather than the underlying processes or observable activities involved.
