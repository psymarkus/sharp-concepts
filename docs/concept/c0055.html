Social learning activities

IRI

https://w3id.org/sharp/concept/SocialLearningActivities

Identifier

c0055

Preferred label

Social learning activities

Alternative labels

No alternative labels available.

Definition

Social learning activities refer to observable actions such as communication, collaboration, coordination or any other interaction with present others in support of learning. The the presence of others can be actual, implied or imagined.
