Intervention outcomes

IRI

https://w3id.org/sharp/concept/c0033

Identifier

c0033

Preferred label

Intervention outcomes

Alternative labels

  • Outcomes of intervening

Definition

Intervention outcomes refer to the quality and appropriateness of selected and implemented interventions as well as the sufficiency of supporting arguments used to justify them.
