Diagnostic outcome

IRI

https://w3id.org/sharp/concept/c0032

Identifier

c0032

Preferred label

Diagnostic outcome

Alternative labels

  • Outcomes of diagnosing

Definition

Diagnostic outcomes refer to the quality of diagnostic judgments, including their accuracy, appropriateness, and the sufficiency of supporting arguments.
