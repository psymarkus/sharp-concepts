Definition

Diagnosing refers to the professional practice of making judgments about clients, situations, or problems based on available information, with the goal of reducing diagnostic uncertainty. Diagnosing may occur individually or collaboratively. It describes the broader phenomenon of diagnosis rather than the underlying processes or observable activities involved.
