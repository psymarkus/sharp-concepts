Definition

Facets of knowledge refer to content-based categories of professional knowledge. Examples from teaching include the facets content knowledge (subject-specific knowledge), pedagogical knowledge (general knowledge about teaching and learning), and pedagogical content knowledge (knowledge about subject-specific teaching strategies).
