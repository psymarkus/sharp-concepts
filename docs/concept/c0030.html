Motivational-affective learning prerequisites

c0030

Preferred label

Motivational-affective learning prerequisites

Alternative labels

No alternative labels available.

Definition

Motivational-affective prerequisites are relatively enduring, yet context-sensitive expectancy–value beliefs and emotional dispositions that learners hold toward a learning domain, activity, and its outcomes.
