Preferred label

Intervention reasoning

Alternative labels

  • Processes of intervening

Definition

Intervention reasoning refers to the cognitive processes involved in generating, evaluating, selecting, and adapting possible courses of action based on diagnostic information, with the aim of identifying and optimizing appropriate and effective responses to a given problem.
