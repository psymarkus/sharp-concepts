Preferred label

Approximation of practice

Alternative labels

No alternative labels available.

Definition

Approximation of practice refers to opportunities to engage in practices of authentic professional practice in a learning environment or activity while reducing complexity, risk, or other constraints of real-world practice. Approximations of practice vary in their degree of similarity and proximitiy to professional practice.
