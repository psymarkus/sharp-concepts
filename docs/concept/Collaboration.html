Definition

Collaboration is a [joint / coordinated / synchronous] activity of at least two individuals to achieve a [common goal / desirable outcome] by pooling knowledge and skills. Relevant facets of collaboration are social interdependence, task division, the introduction of new ideas, the sharing of information, resources, and knowledge as well as good communication.
