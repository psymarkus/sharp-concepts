Definition

Feedback is information given to learners during or after their interaction with the simulation. The goal is to inform learners about a discrepancy between their current task performance (e.g. diagnosing or intervention) and the (instructionally) desired performance. Feedback can be human or computer generated. Feedback in simulations can be static (simple or elaborated), simple adaptive feedback (task level), or elaborated adaptive feedback (process level or self-regulation level).
